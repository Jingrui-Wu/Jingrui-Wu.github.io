Two perturbations together are mostly the sum of each alone. We model only the part that doesn't add up.

At a glance

Problem

Predict how cells respond to gene pairs that were never measured.

Idea

Start from the additive guess and learn only the interaction.

Result

+18% synergy correlation over the best flow-matching baseline.

How it works

  1. 1Additive startFlow begins at control + effect A + effect B.
  2. 2Residual flowAll capacity goes to the non-additive part.
  3. 3Gene-aware encoderA gene–gene mask plus a learned, perturbation-specific adjustment.
  4. 4Population matchingMMD aligns cell distributions, since cells are unpaired.

Results

+18%synergy correlation
+11%DE-gene Spearman
0.568 → 0.631
+19%non-additive gene recall
−7%L2 error

Held-out double perturbations (Norman), vs. scDFM. Gains carry over to drug combinations (ComboSciPlex).
