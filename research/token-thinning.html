Medical imaging

Dynamic token thinning for Alzheimer's disease

  • 2026.09 – present
  • Rice Statistics · with Cindy Zhang

Most of a brain MRI says little about Alzheimer's. The model shouldn't spend the same effort on every patch.

At a glance

Problem

Vision transformers process every patch in every layer.

Idea

Learn which patches to drop, and stop early when confident.

Status

In progress: CN vs. AD classification on ADNI.

How it works

  1. 1ScoreEach layer rates every patch.
  2. 2Keep top-kDifferentiable (Gumbel straight-through) selection.
  3. 3Exit earlyConfident cases finish in fewer layers.
  4. 4DistillA full-patch teacher guides the thinned student.

Data pipeline

  • 431 ADNI subjects
  • N4 bias correction
  • SynthStrip skull stripping
  • ANTs registration to MNI
  • subject-level splits

My role

Developing the model; built the MRI preprocessing pipeline.
