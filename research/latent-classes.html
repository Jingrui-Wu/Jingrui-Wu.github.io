Same diagnosis, different disease underneath. Can we find the hidden subgroups?

At a glance

Problem

Clustering confuses real subgroups with age or genotype effects.

Idea

Model class membership and biomarkers separately, with no distribution assumed.

Status

Manuscript in preparation.

How it works

  1. 1Class modelCovariates such as APOE4 set class probabilities.
  2. 2Biomarker modelEach biomarker, flexibly rescaled, regresses on covariates such as age.
  3. 3EMEEAlternate class updates with estimating equations.

Now applying it to ADNI

  • CSF p-tau
  • CSF Aβ42
  • MRI hippocampal volume
  • adjusted for age and APOE4

My role

Designed and ran the simulation studies (binary and three-class; vs. K-means, GMM and Ward). Now working on the ADNI application.
