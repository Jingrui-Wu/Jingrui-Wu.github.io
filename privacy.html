Privacy and analytics

This personal academic website is maintained by Jingrui Wu. Optional analytics help me understand which research pages people visit and how they find the site.

Your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you select Accept analytics. You can decline and use the entire website. Use Analytics settings at the bottom of any page to change your choice. Your browser remembers your choice for up to 180 days.

What is collected

If you accept, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, interactions, approximate location, device and browser information, and referral sources. Analytics cookies on this site are limited to 180 days. I use these statistics to understand visits; they do not provide a list of visitors' names or email addresses.

The site's Analytics configuration disables Google signals and advertising personalization. It removes query strings and fragments from the page and referrer URLs supplied to Analytics.

Google processes this information. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and page resources

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Some pages also load fonts from Google Fonts. These services are separate from optional Analytics. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
